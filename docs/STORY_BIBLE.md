# Ninjago: Fifth Student — Story Bible (Chapters 1–3)

Source: owner's script PDF "Ninjago: Fifth Student — Echo, Broken Blade & Chapters 1-3" (Oct 2, 2026), transcribed to plain text so tools can read it.

The script content below is the owner's. Items tagged **[CANON CHECK]** or **[SPEC GAP]** were added during transcription. They are open questions, not part of the original script. Each has a default in `PRD.md` §10.

---

## 0. Premise

- Alternate-reality Ninjago that follows the show's canon from the pilots through Crystalized. Dragons Rising characters (Arin, Sora, Wyldfyre, Riyu, Ras, Beatrix and the rest of the DR cast) do not exist in this reality.
- The player is **Wu's fifth student**, an original character, training beside Kai, Jay, Zane and Cole from the pilots. Name and appearance are TBD.
- The player's element is **Echo**, a new element. They carry a cracked blade, the **Hollow Edge**.
- Secret heritage: part Oni, part Dragon, unknown to the player. It is a burden (access and cost), never a source of victory. It is **not stated outright until Season 8 content**, which is outside v1.
- Fixed rule: the player, the ghost and the blade never land a killing blow on a canon villain. The ninja keep their big moments.
- Fixed points are canon outcomes that happen in every branch. Flex points are everything around them (who fights beside you, who you save, who trusts you).
- v1 scope is Chapters 1–3. Season 2 was originally the planned end of the whole show, so Chapters 1–3 make a complete first release.

## 1. Echo: the element

Echo replaces Salt as the player's element. Your last action replays half a second later as a ghost copy, so every move can be doubled, delayed or redirected.

- **Core rule:** the game records the last **1.5 seconds** of input. Echo moves replay it from a ghost (a faint afterimage of the player sprite).
- **Resonance meter:** **4 pips**. Gain a pip by landing hits and perfect dodges. Echo moves spend pips.
- **Ghost limit:** 1 ghost at a time in Chapters 1–2; 2 ghosts from Chapter 3.
- **Ghost damage:** 50% of the player's. The ghost cannot land a killing blow on a canon villain (same rule as the player).
- **Weakness:** if an enemy hits the ghost, it dissolves and you lose 1 pip. Good players bait enemies into wasting hits on it.
- **Lore tie:** Echo is the trace left by the First Spinjitzu Master crossing between realms. Wu does not recognise it, which is why he calls the player's spinjitzu "unstable" in Chapter 1.
- **[CANON CHECK]** No canon element called Echo is known; verify on the Ninjago wiki before locking. Salt can return as a Serpentine ally's element.

### 1.1 Echo movesets (12 moves, 3 roles)

Costs are Resonance pips.

**Mobility**

| Move | What it does | Cost | Unlocks |
|---|---|---|---|
| Afterstep | Dash. The ghost repeats the dash 0.4s later along the same path and acts as a decoy. | 0 | Ch 1 |
| Rewind Step | Tap dash again within 1s to snap back to where the ghost stands. | 1 | Ch 1 |
| Echo Leap | Jump; the ghost repeats it, so you can double-jump from the ghost's position over gaps. | 1 | Ch 2 |
| Phase Swap | Swap places with the ghost, passing through thin barriers and bars. | 2 | Ch 3 |

**Offense**

| Move | What it does | Cost | Unlocks |
|---|---|---|---|
| Twin Strike | The last hit of any combo repeats 0.5s later from the ghost. | 0 | Ch 1 |
| Echo Spin | Your spinjitzu leaves a ghost spin where you started, so enemies get pinched between two vortexes. | 2 | Ch 2 |
| Delayed Volley | Blade slash waves and thrown items fire a second time from the ghost. | 1 | Ch 2 |
| Resonant Shatter | Enemies hit by both you and the ghost within 1s are staggered and take a burst. It staggers, never finishes. | 3 | Ch 3 |

**Defense**

| Move | What it does | Cost | Unlocks |
|---|---|---|---|
| Decoy Veil | Stand still for 1s: the ghost taunts, and enemies target it for 3s. | 0 | Ch 1 |
| Hollow Guard | Block, and the ghost repeats it in a radius, making a parry zone that reflects projectiles (strong vs Venomari). | 1 | Ch 2 |
| Counter-Echo | A perfect parry stores the enemy's attack, then replays it at them 1s later at 50% damage (strong vs Anacondrai). **[CANON CHECK]** see §5 tribe note. | 2 | Ch 2 |
| Sound Wall | All ghosts form a barrier that blocks projectiles and shields civilians. This is the rescue move. | 3 | Ch 3 |

- **[SPEC GAP]** The script says Chapter 1 gives one move per role, but Rewind Step also unlocks in Chapter 1, so Chapter 1 actually has four moves (two mobility). Default: keep all four.

### 1.2 Design notes

- **Skill ceiling:** chain roles. Example: Afterstep to pull aggro, Counter-Echo as they swing, Resonant Shatter as the ghost lands the second hit.
- **Ninja trust perks:** Kai adds a fire flicker to Twin Strike. Cole makes Hollow Guard heavier. Zane shows enemy attack patterns on the ghost's path. Jay (owner's suggestion): shorter Rewind Step window. Nya's guarding perk arrives when she joins. **[CANON CHECK]** Nya joins the team in Season 5, which is outside v1, so the Nya perk is deferred.

## 2. The Broken Blade: the Hollow Edge

The super weapon is a cracked blade that only heals as you earn trust, and its full power costs you. It rings like a struck bell when drawn, which is why Echo suits it.

| Stage | Chapter | Form | New ability | What triggers it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Shard | Ch 1 | Half a blade, no edge. Hums near hidden paths and ghosts. | none | Found off the Golden Weapon route |
| 2. Tuned | Ch 2 | Reforged edge, cracks sealed with the colour of your top Serpentine tribe | Wave Slash (ranged, doubled by Delayed Volley) | Your highest faction standing |
| 3. Resonant | Ch 3 | Full blade, still one deep crack | Hollow Verdict: a charged cut that staggers a boss for a rescue window | Choosing to push it on the Dark Island |
| 4. Awakened | Ch 5+ | Whole blade | Scoped later; ties to the Oni/Dragon origin | Heritage reveal |

Stage 4 is out of v1 scope.

### 2.1 Corruption meter (0–100)

Hollow Verdict and every full-power use raise it. It is the cost of power.

- **25:** the blade hums audibly and some NPCs avoid you.
- **50:** enemies mark you first, so you draw more aggro.
- **75:** your ghost acts on its own now and then.
- **100:** forced stagger, and a story consequence in that chapter.

**Lowering it:** rescues, keeping a promise to an ally, and resting at the monastery. This keeps "you carry the cost" as a mechanic.

### 2.2 Origin

The blade is a piece of the tool the First Spinjitzu Master used to cross between realms. Both sides tried to destroy it, so it was hidden broken. It answers you because of your heritage, and the story never says so outright until Season 8.

## 4. Chapter 1: The Fifth Student (Pilots)

**Fixed points:** the Golden Weapons are gathered; Garmadon escapes.
**Flex:** who guards which weapon, who you help, who you tell about the blade.

**[CANON CHECK]** Pilot canon, as read from the episode summaries: Wu hid the four Golden Weapons with a guardian dragon at each site. The Scythe of Quakes is in the Caves of Despair with an earth dragon. The Sword of Fire is at the Fire Temple. The ninja recover three weapons, the Skulkin lead is Samukai, the finale is in the Underworld, and Garmadon escapes through a portal opened by the weapons' combined power. Verify scene locations and names against this before building.

- **Scene 1: The monastery, dawn (tutorial).** Wu drills you beside Kai, Jay, Zane and Cole. You learn movement, basic combo and block. Wu: "Your spinjitzu doesn't spin true. It trails." Your first spin leaves a faint ghost, which teaches Afterstep and Twin Strike.
- **Scene 2: Sparring partner (choice).** Wu pairs you with one of the four. This starts that ninja's trust track at +2. Kai: fast, aggressive. Cole: heavy, patient. Zane: precise, quiet. Jay: chatty, fun; he teases your trailing spin, and it sticks as a joke.
- **Scene 3: The Skulkin threat.** News reaches the monastery: Skulkin are moving on the weapon route. Wu sends the team to secure the Golden Weapons first. You get Decoy Veil during a short village defence.
- **Scene 4: The first weapon hunt.** A trap-and-puzzle dungeon built to teach Rewind Step. At the end you meet a stone guardian dragon, and it doesn't attack you. The others notice. Flag: `dragon_ignored_you`.
- **Scene 5: Weapon guard (choice).** Wu asks who guards which weapon. You pick one weapon to watch with one ninja, and that pairing becomes your ally in the next fight. Whoever you did not pick finds the other trust track cooler for now.
- **Scene 6: The Shard (optional path).** Off the route, a hidden chamber. The blade hums when you stand close. You take half a broken blade (Stage 1 of the Hollow Edge). Choice: tell Wu now, or keep it quiet. Flag: `wu_knows_blade`.
- **Scene 7: The Skulkin general (boss).** A large Skulkin fights your party at the final weapon site. Use Twin Strike and Decoy Veil to hold his attention while the ninja deal the finishing blows. The boss is beaten by the team, not by you. **[CANON CHECK]** The canon Skulkin leader is Samukai. Default is an unnamed original "Skulkin General", not Samukai.
- **Scene 8: Garmadon escapes (cost scene).** In the confusion Garmadon slips away. You are closest, and you choose: chase him, or stay and pull villagers out of a collapsing building. Garmadon escapes either way (fixed point). Chasing loses the rescue and gives extra trust to whichever ninja sees you leave. Staying costs nothing in canon but raises village standing and lowers corruption by 10.
- **Scene 9: Wu at night.** With all the weapons gathered, Wu watches you train alone and says your trail has a name he doesn't know. If `wu_knows_blade` is true, he asks to see it and says nothing more. The chapter ends on the blade humming faintly in your pack.

## 5. Chapter 2: Whispers in the Sand (Rise of the Snakes)

**Fixed points:** Lloyd is the Green Ninja; Garmadon kills the Great Devourer.
**Flex:** which Serpentine tribes learn to trust you. This chapter introduces faction standing and the gossip hub.

- **Scene 1: Ninjago City hub.** The city is nervous. You open the gossip system here: rumours change after each mission, and NPCs offer different quests based on what they've heard. Two starting rumours: "the fifth student can't control their trail" and "Wu is hiding something".
- **Scene 2: The tombs open.** The Serpentine wake. You get Echo Leap in a collapsing tomb. Pick up the first tribe contact on the way out.
- **Scene 3: Four tribes, four tests.** Each tribe has a trust quest and a combat style. Helping one tribe's quest raises its standing and lowers another's.
- **Scene 4: The Tuning (blade Stage 2).** The cracks in your Shard react to the tribe that trusts you most. You reforge it at the monastery forge, and the new edge takes that tribe's colour. This unlocks Wave Slash. Flag: `blade_tribe`.
- **Scene 5: Lloyd's prophecy.** The Green Ninja is revealed (fixed point). Your reaction is a choice: back him early, or train harder alone. Backing him raises Kai/Zane/Jay/Cole trust; training alone adds a Resonance pip and slows the team's trust by one level. **[SPEC GAP]** "adds a Resonance pip" is ambiguous (current vs maximum). Default: start Chapter 3 with +1 current pip; the 4-pip maximum does not change.
- **Scene 6: Ally or enemy.** Tribe standings now decide who fights alongside the ninja at the Devourer. A tribe at high standing sends a scout or warrior; a hostile tribe sends an ambush on the road.
- **Scene 7: The Great Devourer (boss).** You can't kill it. You pin its tail segments with Echo Spin and Counter-Echo while civilians are pulled out. Garmadon lands the killing strike (fixed point). Your Sound Wall isn't unlocked yet, so you use the ghost as a decoy for fleeing villagers.
- **Scene 8: The elder's question.** A Serpentine elder watches your ghost trail after the fight and quietly asks where you learned it. Choice: admit you don't know, or deflect. It decides whether that tribe passes a rumour about your trail along in Chapter 3.
- **Scene 9: Epilogue.** The rumour board updates based on what you did, and the city treats the ninja a little differently because of it.

### 5.1 Tribe table (as written in the script)

| Tribe | Threat type | Your counter | Trust quest |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hypnobrai | Hypnosis; dodge or block | Decoy Veil pulls the stare off you | Free a captured scout |
| Fangpyre | Venom bites; dodge | Rewind Step out of range | Treat a poisoned elder |
| Venomari | Projectiles | Hollow Guard reflects them | Escort a trader |
| Anacondrai | Heavy punish | Counter-Echo | Prove you won't run |

**[CANON CHECK]** This is the most important canon issue in the script. Season 1 has **five** Serpentine tribes: Hypnobrai, Fangpyre, Venomari, Constrictai and Anacondrai. The Anacondrai are not an available tribe in Season 1, because Pythor is its last survivor. The Anacondrai generals return in Season 4. Default: replace Anacondrai with **Constrictai** as the fourth playable tribe (heavy grappling, "heavy punish" role), and use Pythor as the Season 1 antagonist. Keep tribe data swappable. Counter-Echo's "strong vs" note then points at Constrictai.

## 6. Chapter 3: The Dark Island (Legacy)

**Fixed points:** the Overlord returns; Lloyd becomes the Golden Ninja.
**Flex:** who is safe on the Dark Island. This chapter ends the first release.

**[CANON CHECK]** Season 2 canon: the ninja lose their elemental powers once the Golden Weapons are gone, and get Elemental Swords at the Temple of Light on the Dark Island (mid-season). The Overlord's army is the Stone Army, and the Garmatron spreads Dark Matter. Decide whether Chapter 3 shows the powers loss and the Temple of Light restoration. Default: yes, as a short beat before Scene 4.

- **Scene 1: Payoffs.** The hub reflects Chapter 2. Tribes you helped turn up as allies, hostile ones turn up as threats, and neutral ones watch. Rumours about you have grown, and one of them is true.
- **Scene 2: The Overlord stirs.** Dark Matter spreads. When it touches you it only partly takes hold (heritage hint 2). Choice: hide it, or tell Wu. Hiding keeps team trust steady but starts the corruption meter at 15 instead of 0.
- **Scene 3: Planning the assault.** The team splits tasks. You choose which ally travels with you based on who you've bonded with, and that ninja's perk is active for the whole chapter.
- **Scene 4: Dark Island, outer ring.** Phase Swap tutorial: you cross barred gates by swapping with your ghost. You get your second ghost here.
- **Scene 5: Who is safe (choice).** Dark Matter is overtaking three groups: captured civilians, an allied Serpentine patrol, and a team of ninja scouts. You can reach two. Sound Wall protects the group you choose. The one you skip is lost for this chapter, and its absence is visible in later chapters' rumours. Zane's choice here sets up his Chapter 4 sacrifice: if you saved the scouts, he is more willing to take the risk later. **[SPEC GAP]** "Reach two" vs "protects the group you choose" is ambiguous. Default: you can reach two of three; Sound Wall shields the one you reach first; the skipped group is lost.
- **Scene 6: The Stage 3 choice.** In the ruins you find the rest of your blade. You may push it now (Resonant, unlocks Hollow Verdict) or leave it cracked. Pushing it adds 30 corruption. This is the only way to unlock the final blade ability in this chapter.
- **Scene 7: The Overlord's guardians (boss).** A multi-phase fight. Resonant Shatter staggers the shield-bearer, so the ninja can break the guard. You can't land the killing hit on any canon villain.
- **Scene 8: Lloyd's transformation.** Lloyd becomes the Golden Ninja (fixed point). You hold the line with Sound Wall while his energy builds. Hollow Verdict, if unlocked, creates a stagger window that lets him strike.
- **Scene 9: Collapse and aftermath.** The Overlord falls. If corruption is over 75, you black out, and Wu sees the blade's mark on your hand. If it is lower, you walk out and keep your secret a little longer. Heritage hints so far: the guardian dragons, and Dark Matter's partial hold.
- **Epilogue hook.** On the way home your ghost lingers one second longer than it should, and for a moment it isn't repeating you.

## 7. What is canon and what is invented

- **Canon (from research):** the Golden Weapons and their guardian dragons, Samukai's Skulkin, the five Serpentine tribes, Pythor, the Great Devourer, the Green Ninja prophecy, the Overlord on the Dark Island, the Golden Ninja, the Temple of Light and Zane's Season 3 sacrifice.
- **Invented by the owner:** the fifth student, Echo, the Hollow Edge and corruption, the rescue groups on the Dark Island (civilians, an allied patrol, ninja scouts), the collapsing-building rescue, the gossip board and the tribe trust quests.
- **Invented in planning:** the Oni/Dragon second-crossing origin of the blade and the player.
